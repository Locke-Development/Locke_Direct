01 / THE PRODUCT

Start with the situation, not the legal terminology.

A person may know they are selling a car, renting a home, hiring a contractor or planning an estate—but not which legal document to prepare. Locke Direct begins with that problem, then provides the appropriate level of guidance.

The key architectural choice: Ask Locke helps explain and recommend, while maintained document clauses and rule-driven assembly control the final legal language.

That separation helps keep product interaction understandable while making the drafting workflow inspectable and predictable.

02 / ENGINEERING CAPABILITIES

Consent-gated marketing analytics on approved public pages, excluding document text, personal form answers and assistant messages.
