## Ask Locke: discover the right document

A customer may describe a situation rather than guessing a legal title, such as selling a vehicle, renting a home, appointing someone to act, or planning an estate. **Ask Locke's document-picker mode** narrows that need to an existing product in the available catalog. It does not create an invented SKU or produce a free-form legal instrument.

When a customer begins a particular form, **field-help mode** can explain a question in plain English and, from facts the customer chooses to share, suggest values for specific allowed fields. Suggestions are checked against the form's actual field IDs and value constraints.

## What makes the approach technically distinct?

- **Escalating, intentional guidance:** the tier represents how much help and scope the transaction needs, not the apparent legal weight of the finished document.
- **Explanatory questions:** questions are meant to explain consequences and prompt for facts needed by the maintained document structure.
- **Jurisdiction-aware decisions:** products apply state and, where required, more local context to the supported content path.
- **Deterministic drafting:** maintained text, rule evaluation, validation and customer selections determine final document language, instead of trusting a chat model to improvise it.
- **Bounded assistant:** catalog allowlisting, field-ID validation, provider budget controls, request limits and a safe unavailable response keep Ask Locke in a support role.
- **Privacy boundary:** the model receives voluntarily supplied chat and limited functional context, not an automatic dump of the complete questionnaire; assistant messages are excluded from analytics.

## What it is not

Ask Locke does not provide legal advice, independent attorney review, or an unrestricted AI-generated contract. Document availability, supported jurisdictions and statutory formalities vary by product. The assistant and provider can fail or misunderstand requests.
