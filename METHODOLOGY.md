## Comparability rules

**Lead** is a narrow, specific advantage over a described competitor offer—not an overall ranking. **Tie** means a similar benefit is published by another provider. **Not verified** means a vendor's available materials do not establish whether it offers an equivalent capability; it must never be presented as absence.

Vendor marketing and price pages are time-sensitive; billing details may differ by jurisdiction, plan, promotion or checkout. LawDepot and eForms explicitly offer non-subscription single-document purchases, so it is inaccurate to imply subscription is mandatory on every rival platform. Legal Templates advertises its own AI generator; it is not accurate to claim Ask Locke is the only AI-supported service.

## Verification boundaries

This presentation does not assert that Locke is more legally accurate, current, legally valid in every state, secure or comprehensive than its competitors. Engineering content readiness checks are **not** substantive attorney review. The source catalog is not a guarantee that every listed document is enabled in production. No independent head-to-head legal validation, live purchase test, AI benchmark or comparative security audit was executed specifically for this publication.

**FormSwift is excluded from the active competitor set:** the company states it [closed in September 2026](https://formswift.com/lp/goodbye).
