## Ask Locke: what customers actually receive

**Before starting:** Describe the transaction in everyday language. Ask Locke selects from actually available products, can clarify ambiguity, and shows a document recommendation with a path directly into its workflow.

**While completing:** Ask Locke can explain individual questions and options, identify information needed for that form, and suggest entries from customer-provided facts. The user explicitly chooses whether to apply suggested values. Its field output is checked against existing form identifiers; it does not silently fill arbitrary fields.

**After assistance:** The document assembly engine—not Ask Locke's free-form chat response—selects and renders maintained clauses and conditional content. **Ask Locke is a document picker and form assistant, not an attorney or an open-ended legal-advice chatbot.** It can be unavailable or misunderstand a request.

See [Ask Locke and guided workflows](ASK-LOCKE-AND-GUIDED-WORKFLOWS.md), [AI boundaries](AI-BOUNDARIES.md), and [document assembly](DOCUMENT-ASSEMBLY.md).

## Why choose Locke?

- **Straightforward ownership:** pay once at one of four published tiers; no automatic subscription conversion.
- **Help finding and completing the document:** Ask Locke operates at both the discovery and in-form stages, including user-approved application of suggested field values.
- **Your facts shape the provisions:** structured questions, maintained conditional clauses, state context and more extensive contingency checks in higher tiers.
- **Revisions are included:** one on eligible free forms and unlimited for 30 days on paid documents.
- **Keep editable output:** PDF and DOCX downloads, with signing where supported.

## Claims deliberately excluded

This review **does not claim** Locke has the most up-to-date, legally accurate, comprehensive or most secure documents on the market. The repository includes state packs, official-source tracking and change checks, but no independently verified nationwide freshness or legal-accuracy superiority benchmark. Competitors provide customization, AI functions, revision access and state-specific documents as well; these are not exclusively Locke capabilities. Product availability is controlled by the current live catalog, not by a repository title count.
